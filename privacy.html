Privacy policy.

Last updated October 20, 2026

The short version. We collect what you give us when you request a spot, use it to answer you and run the masterclass, and never sell it. You can ask us to correct or delete your information at any time by emailing hello@apexfitness.com.

1. Who we are

ApexFitness (“ApexFitness,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Apex Strength Lab in SoHo, New York, and the website at apexfitness.com. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through this website, our reservation form, and our email communications.

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at hello@apexfitness.com or write to ApexFitness, Apex Strength Lab, SoHo, New York, NY 10012.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Reservation details: your name, email address, optional phone number, training goal, experience level, and any note you add for the coaches.
  • Communication: the content of emails, messages, and questions you send us.
  • Enrollment and payment: if you choose to enroll, our payment provider collects billing details. We receive confirmation of payment and limited transaction details, not your full card number.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, referring page, and general location derived from your IP address.
  • Usage data: pages viewed, links clicked, and interactions with the reservation form. We use this in aggregate to improve the site and measure interest in the masterclass.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: small files that help the site function, remember preferences, and—where enabled—measure traffic. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

3. How we use your information

  • Respond to your reservation request and confirm availability.
  • Prepare for the masterclass, including pairing athletes with the right coach and scaling the sessions.
  • Send operational emails such as confirmation, preparation details, schedule changes, and follow-up resources.
  • Send occasional updates about future cohorts and ApexFitness programs, where permitted. You can opt out at any time.
  • Process payments, maintain records, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.
  • Protect the site and our community from spam, fraud, and abuse.
  • Understand what is working on the site and improve the experience.

4. Legal bases and consent

Where required by law, we process your information based on your consent, our legitimate interest in operating and improving the masterclass, the performance of a contract with you, and compliance with legal obligations. You may withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that already took place.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the website and masterclass, and only as needed to do that work. These may include hosting, email delivery, analytics, payment processing, and scheduling providers.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect the safety of participants or staff, to enforce our terms, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business. If that happens, we will take reasonable steps to ensure your information remains protected.

6. How long we keep it

We keep reservation and enrollment records for as long as needed to run the masterclass and maintain business records, then for the period required by applicable law. Marketing contacts are kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. Analytics data is retained in aggregate or for a limited period.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including encrypted connections, restricted access, and limited retention. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please avoid sending sensitive health or financial information through the reservation form.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information; to object to or restrict certain processing; to withdraw consent; and to opt out of marketing. California residents may also have the right to know what we collect and to request deletion, subject to legal exceptions. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising as those terms are defined under California law.

To make a request, email hello@apexfitness.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. We may need to verify your identity before acting. You can also unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message.

9. Cookies and analytics

Essential cookies may be used to keep the site secure and remember your form progress. If we enable analytics or advertising tools, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent first. You can block or delete cookies in your browser; some site features may not work as intended.

10. Children

The masterclass and this website are intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, email us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal requirements change. The “last updated” date at the top shows when it changed. Material changes will be communicated by email or a notice on this site where appropriate.
