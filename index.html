Questions, answered

The things people usually ask.

Still unsure? Email a coach directly. A real person—usually Maya—will get back to you within one business day.

Is this for beginners or experienced lifters?

Both. Every drill is scaled to your level, and you’ll be grouped with athletes at a similar stage. If you can move without acute pain and want to train better, you belong in the room. Complete beginners are welcome; experienced lifters finally get the details they’ve been missing.

What if I can’t attend all three days?

The three sessions build on one another, so attendance matters. If plans change, you can transfer your registration once to the next cohort up to seven days before the start date. Just email us.

Do I need to be “in shape” to join?

No. You need to be able to attend three two-hour sessions and move without acute pain. We scale load and range to you. If you’re returning from an injury, check with your clinician first and tell us what you’re working with.

What exactly is included for $299?

Six hours of in-person coaching with Maya and Andre, the 90-Day Strength Blueprint, the Performance Nutrition Playbook, the Apex Movement Library, the Home Equipment Guide, and access to the private member community. The full kit is valued at $376 and is included.

What is the cancellation and refund policy?

Cancel more than seven days before the first session for a full refund. Inside seven days, you can transfer your spot to another cohort once. After the masterclass, the 14-day Apex Standard guarantee applies—attend all three sessions, do the work, and if it wasn’t worth it, email us for a refund.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Pay $299 in full or split it into two payments of $159. Choose the plan on the pricing card and we’ll send the secure payment details after we confirm your spot.

Where is the masterclass held?

At Apex Strength Lab in SoHo, New York. We’ll send the exact address, transit notes, and what to bring in your confirmation email. You’ll need training shoes, water, and a notebook; chalk and equipment are provided.

Will I be sore? Will I be able to train after?

You may feel some new things—that’s normal when you change positions. We manage volume carefully so you leave stimulated, not wrecked. Sunday includes recovery and progression rules, and the 90-day plan builds in deloads.
